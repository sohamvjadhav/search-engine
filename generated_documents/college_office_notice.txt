OFFICE NOTICE

Subject: Submission of Semester Fee Receipts and ID Card Verification

All students of First Year, Second Year, Third Year, and Final Year are
hereby informed that they must submit a copy of their Semester Fee
Receipt along with their College ID Card for verification to the College
Office.

Details are as follows:

Submission Venue: College Office, Administrative Block (Room No. 102)
Submission Dates: 01 February 2026 to 05 February 2026 Timings: 10:30 AM
to 4:30 PM (except Sunday)

Students who fail to complete the above process within the stipulated
time may face difficulties in:

-   Hall ticket generation for internal/external examinations
-   Access to library and laboratory facilities
-   Participation in college activities

Students are therefore advised to strictly adhere to the schedule and
complete the verification process at the earliest.

For any queries, contact the College Office during working hours.
