Samarth Group of Institutions - College of Engineering
Belhe, Maharashtra, India

Document Type: Institutional profile
Last Updated: 2026-03-27

Overview
Samarth Group of Institutions at Belhe is a regional engineering-focused campus serving undergraduate learners in core and emerging technology branches.

Mission
To provide practical, industry-aware technical education with emphasis on employability, discipline, and problem-solving skills.

Campus Highlights
- Academic blocks for engineering departments
- Central library with digital reading access
- Basic hostel and transport support
- Industry talks and training workshops

Programs Offered
- Computer Engineering
- Civil Engineering
- Mechanical Engineering
- E&TC Engineering
- Artificial Intelligence and Data Science
